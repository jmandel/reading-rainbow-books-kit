## About the book

*Tight Times* (Viking, 1979) is written by Barbara Shook Hazen with pictures by Trina Schart Hyman. A little boy tells the story himself. He wants a dog, and the answer is always no. His father explains that "tight times" are the reason: the family eats "Mr. Bulk" instead of cereal in little boxes, goes to the sprinkler instead of the lake, and eats soupy things with lima beans instead of Sunday roast beef. Then a hard day at home changes everything, and the boy finds a pet in a place nobody expected.

It suits a listener who knows how it feels to want something and hear "not now." Grown-ups will notice what the boy only half understands. Reviewers at the time disagreed about the mood. *Booklist* said the story "has a sweet, sad poignancy," and Zena Sutherland of the *Bulletin of the Center for Children's Books* found the monologue "convincingly that of a child." Kirkus found it too bleak. If money worries or a lost job are close to home, it can be a gentle way to start talking.

## Author and illustrator

**Barbara Shook Hazen** was born in 1930 in Dayton, Ohio, and studied at Smith College and Columbia University. She worked at *Ladies' Home Journal* and then as a children's book editor at Western Publishing before she began writing full time in 1960. Her *Even If I Did Something Awful* won a Christopher Award in 1981.

**Trina Schart Hyman** (1939–2004) was born in Philadelphia and illustrated more than 150 books, many of them fairy tales and legends. She won the Caldecott Medal for *Saint George and the Dragon*, retold by Margaret Hodges, and Caldecott Honors for books including her own *Little Red Riding Hood* and *Hershel and the Hanukkah Goblins*. From 1973 to 1979 she was art director of *Cricket*, a magazine for children.

## In this episode

LeVar Burton gives the lead-in: some kids just can't have pets, and a "no" hurts whatever the reason. Then the book is read by Patrick McCaffrey, who is credited as narrator but not named on air. Listen for a little soap opera playing on Mrs. Macintosh's television partway through.

The full TV episode, this series' very first, also had parts that are not in the podcast audio. It opened at a pet show where every pet won a ribbon, including a pair of pet rocks and an imaginary hamster. After the reading, LeVar and a girl named Hillary found ways to have fun without spending money, from rag basketball to sock puppets, and ended up at the free public library.
