## About the book

Dolores is a loud, lively little girl who spots a sign in a window: a four-year-old cat named Duncan needs a home. She wants him at once, and her older sister Faye can only sigh, "Poor Duncan." The next day Duncan arrives in a carrying case, shoots out, and disappears under a cabinet.

From there the story is about patience. Dolores has plans for her new pet. She wants to play dress-up, teach him to fetch, and have him all to herself. Duncan has other ideas, and he seems to prefer Faye. Dolores, who is not used to being second choice, has to decide what to do about it.

Along the way come a cape, a lovely hat, a hiding place built from chairs and a blanket, and a tea party with Martha and Mabel. It suits children who have a new pet, a shy friend, or a sibling who always seems to be the favourite. Duncan and Dolores was published in 1986 and won a Christopher Award.

## Author and illustrator

Barbara Samuels grew up on Long Island, in a house that always had one or two cats, with two older sisters, Susie and Freya, who were both good at drawing. She has written that drawing was a struggle for her at first, and that trying to capture her cats' moods helped her learn. She studied history at the University of Wisconsin, earned an M.A. at Teachers College, Columbia University, and taught before studying illustration at the School of Visual Arts in Manhattan.

Her illustration work included painting watercolor backgrounds for an animated Raggedy Anne story film and freelance jobs for The New York Times and magazines. A story about two sisters and their cat felt like a natural first picture book.

Dolores and Duncan returned in several more books. Faye and Dolores came first, followed by Happy Birthday, Dolores, What's So Great about Cindy Snappleby?, Aloha, Dolores, Dolores on Her Toes, Happy Valentine's Day, Dolores and Dolores Meets Her Match.
