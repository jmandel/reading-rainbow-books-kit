## About the book

*Pet Stories You Don't Have to Walk* is a SeaStar Books anthology from 2001, a collection of pet stories. The library contents list names seven pieces: Laurie Krasny Brown's "The Best Pet," Lee Bennett Hopkins's "Why Not Get a Pterodactyl," an excerpt from Seymour Reit's *A Dog's Tale*, Cynthia Rylant's "The Kittens," Jack Prelutsky's "My Fish Can Ride a Bicycle," selections from Syd Hoff's *Danny and the Dinosaur*, and Sara Swan Miller's "The Good Day."

The Reading Rainbow episode takes two of them. In the first, a boy named Danny wishes he had a dinosaur to play with, and a voice answers that it would like to play with him. In the second, a puppy tells his own story: he is lonely at the pound until a girl named Wendy comes along, and since she has never had a puppy before, he has a great deal to teach her.

Both are told in short lines and lots of dialogue, and the dinosaur even honks like a car.

## Author and illustrator

The two excerpts have different creators. Syd Hoff (1912-2004) was born in the Bronx. He sold his first cartoon to *The New Yorker* at 18 and eventually sold 571 of them there between 1931 and 1975. He wrote and illustrated more than 60 volumes in HarperCollins's I Can Read series. *Danny and the Dinosaur* (1958) has sold over ten million copies and been translated into a dozen languages. The idea came when Hoff began drawing for one of his daughters during physical therapy. Hoff also wrote *Sammy the Seal*.

Seymour Reit (1918-2001) was born in New York City and wrote more than 80 books, mostly for children. He began as an animator at Fleischer Studios, wrote gags for *Popeye* and *Betty Boop* cartoons, and contributed more than 60 pieces to *Mad*. He also claimed to have created Casper the Friendly Ghost, a claim cartoonist Joe Oriolo disputed. *A Dog's Tale*, with pictures by Kate Flanagan, was first published in 1996.

## In this episode

The host opens with: "Hi. You're just in time to help me find one of my best friends." The friend is Roy, his golden retriever, whose birthday it is, and the host plays hide-and-seek, then gets Roy ready for a bath.

Jason Alexander reads both pieces: selections from *Danny and the Dinosaur*, then, after a short lead-in about being best friends with an animal, an excerpt from *A Dog's Tale*. The audio ends on the puppy's line about snuggling in Wendy's bed and making her so happy.

Not in the podcast audio:
- A man and his African gray parrot, Bubu, at bath time and lunch.
- A visit to an animal shelter on adopting a pet, including Scarlett, a calico cat badly burned in a garage fire while rescuing her kittens.
- A dog bakery, where a pastry chef bakes Roy a dog-safe birthday cake.
- Three children reviewing *Harry and Lulu*, *Aloha, Dolores* and *Moondogs*.
