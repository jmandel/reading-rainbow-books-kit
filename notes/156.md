## About the book
Clarisse is a stray cat who loves books. She cannot read the "dark, squiggly lines," but she loves the pictures. When she spots a library, where people walk in empty-handed and walk out with books, she is determined to get inside. The keeper of the books turns her away, but Clarisse keeps trying.

The story is gentle and quick to follow. It suits children who are just starting to puzzle out letters, and anyone who has felt shut out of something they badly wanted to join. Kirkus calls it "Sweetly encouraging" and lists it as a picture book for ages 3 to 8. School Library Journal, in a starred review, called it "catnip for librarians and young book lovers alike."

## Author and illustrator
Lauren Emmons wrote and illustrated the book; it is their first picture book as both author and illustrator. Emmons uses they/them pronouns, grew up in Minnesota and lives in North Dakota with a spouse and two cats. They hold a master's degree in children's book illustration from Anglia Ruskin University in Cambridge, England, and work at a local library.

The idea began around 2019, when Emmons moved to the UK for graduate study and left their cat at home in the United States. Emmons read a news article about a cat that wanted to enter a library, then researched library cats around the world. For a long time the project was called "The Library Cat," and under that title it was shortlisted for the 2020 World Illustration Awards. In an interview, Emmons said Clarisse is based on their own cat, Joline, and that her love of pictures but trouble with words reflects Emmons's own dyslexia.

The book is a Children's Book Council Favorite (2025). Emmons also illustrated *Prak Fills the House* by Donna L. Washington (2023).

## In this episode
The podcast audio opens with the host, Mychal Threets, saying, "Hi, welcome to Reading Rainbow," then talking about how libraries were the first place he felt he belonged. He introduces a library cat, Barnaby Von Whiskers ("This is Barnaby Von Whiskers"; the picture is not part of the audio) and then the book. The reading follows, ending on the last line, "And best of all, every day was filled with stories." Jamie Chung reads the book.

Cut from the podcast audio:
- A quiz of three "what can you remember?" questions about the story.
- A field visit to the Animal Care Center of NYC in Ridgewood, Queens, where children tour the cat, rabbit and dog rooms, learn that a slow blink tells a cat "I trust you," and read to cats.
- The host helping children decorate and fill sidewalk libraries, with a librarian Q&A.
- Three kids' book recommendations from West Orange Public Library.
