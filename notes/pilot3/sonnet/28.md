## About the book
*Feelings* is a picture book with no single storyline. Each page gathers small comic-strip pictures, with speech balloons and captions, of children caught up in one emotion: jealousy when a sibling gets all the attention, pride in a block tower, shyness on the first day in a new class, fear of a story, anger over borrowed crayons. Two birds comment from the sidelines.

It suits toddlers and preschoolers who are starting to put names to what they feel. Booklist's Denise M. Wilms praised its "lighthearted mood" and a "fresh, colorful execution [that] lends grace beyond what often passes for bibliotherapy." The Santa Cruz Sentinel said it "will delight children because it validates their feelings." Not every reviewer agreed: School Library Journal found the number of pictures on each page confusing, and the Bulletin of the Center for Children's Books thought the illustrations too small and used inconsistently. Greenwillow Books published it in 1984.

## Author and illustrator
Aliki, who writes and draws under that single name, made this book on her own. She was born Aliki Liacouras on September 3, 1929, in Wildwood Crest, New Jersey, to parents from Greece. She graduated from the Philadelphia Museum School of Art in 1951, then worked in store display, painted murals, ran a greeting card company and taught art. In 1957 she married the author Franz Brandenberg; they lived in Switzerland, then New York, and from 1977 in London.

Her first book, *The Story of William Tell*, appeared in 1960. Encyclopedia.com notes that she adapts her style to each subject, including comic-strip layouts with word and thought balloons, and that she likes to start a nonfiction book knowing little so she must "get it right." The same source says *Feelings* won the Prix du Livre pour Enfants in Geneva in 1987.

Other picture books by her include *We Are Best Friends*, *All by Myself!*, *Hello! Good-bye!* and *Manners*.

## In this episode
The podcast opens in a park: a child orders ice cream, then a merry-go-round line forms and someone is cut in front of. LeVar's lead-in follows: "Now, you may not be able to see or touch your feelings, but you've got them. And so does everyone else. A whole rainbow of feelings." The skit mentions a character called Blah, whom the podcast never introduces.

The book is read by a group of voices: Raima Evans, Jessica Mercado, Laura Haymann, Paul Sidoriak, Jonathan Rose, Timothy Oakes, Jerry David and Pamela Stogner. The audio ends on a page where a pet named Whiskers has died: "We'll miss you, Whiskers."

Content note: the audio includes a scary dragon story, angry shouting and sadness over a pet's death.

Not in the podcast audio: composer Kirk Nurock leading a group in voice sounds; Dr. Penny Patterson and Coco the gorilla signing about feelings, including grief over a kitten; a friendship song; and kids reviewing books.
