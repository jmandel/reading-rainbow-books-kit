## About the book

*Through Moon and Stars and Night Skies* is a picture book about a boy who comes from far away to meet the parents who will adopt him. He tells the story himself, from the plane, where he holds a photograph of his new family, to the house with a green tree out front. Inside, the rooms are full of strange things, and he yells when he is set down. A red dog, a teddy-bear quilt and a rocking chair by the window are the things he has seen only in his pictures. The book suits young listeners who have made a big move or are getting used to a new home, and who know how a new place can feel frightening before it feels like theirs. Publishers Weekly, reviewing it in March 1990, praised the "gentle watercolors" and called the text "lyrical, thoughtful," and recommended the book for ages 4 to 8.

## Author and illustrator

Ann Warren Turner was born on December 10, 1945, in Northampton, Massachusetts. She earned a study certificate at the University of Oxford in 1966, a B.A. from Bates College in 1967, and a Master in Teaching from the University of Massachusetts in 1968. Her picture books include *Heron Street*, illustrated by Lisa Desimini (1989), and *Katie's Trunk*, illustrated by Ron Himler (1992). The National Council for the Social Studies gave Turner first prize in 1991 for *Through Moon and Stars and Night Skies*, which was published by Harper in 1990.

James Graham Hale illustrated the book. Publishers Weekly, in its March 1990 review, described him as a "newcomer" and praised his "gentle watercolors." A library catalog record lists the book as a 31-page picture book with color illustrations, and as "A Charlotte Zolotow book."

## In this episode

The lead-in is the first in-audio sentence: "Hi. It's family skate day at Prospect Park." Several speakers, none of them LeVar, talk about how families get on each other's nerves and still make you feel great. The book is read by Andrew Lei. The podcast audio ends on the book's last line, "...coming to a room where your arms were always held out to me."

Not in the podcast audio: the Harris family's adoption story, the Peek family's volleyball game and the Abney family's new baby, all shot on location, LeVar's closing remarks about adoption, and three book reviews by children.

Content note: the book includes a child who is frightened on a long flight and in a new home, and who yells.
