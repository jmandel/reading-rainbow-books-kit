## About the book

*Feelings* is a picture book by Aliki about the emotions children know well. In the reading, young characters feel proud, jealous, lonely, shy, angry and scared, and the story moves quickly from one feeling to the next, so a listener can name each one as it comes. The book suits preschool and early-school listeners who are learning the words for what they feel.

Reviewers were mixed. The *Santa Cruz Sentinel* said the book "will delight children because it validates their feelings," which speaks to the book's strength for young listeners. *School Library Journal* found that the number of pictures on each page made the book confusing, and the *Bulletin of the Center for Children's Books* thought the illustrations were too small and used inconsistently. Listeners who enjoy pausing to talk about each scene will get the most from it.

## Author and illustrator

Aliki is the pen name of Aliki Liacouras Brandenberg, born September 3, 1929, in Wildwood Crest, New Jersey. Her parents were from Greece and taught her to speak Greek before English. She grew up in Philadelphia and graduated from the Philadelphia Museum School of Art in 1951. Aliki wrote and illustrated *Feelings* herself, and it is one of many picture books she has made for young readers. In 1991 she won the Pennsylvania School Librarians' Association Award.

Her bibliography runs from fiction to nonfiction, and the Wikipedia list of her books includes *Milk: From Cow to Carton* and *Digging Up Dinosaurs* alongside her picture books. Young listeners who like *Feelings* might enjoy *My Five Senses*, *My Hands* or *Welcome, Little Baby*, all by Aliki, which are on the same list.

## In this episode

The audio begins with a short scene of children in an ice cream line, not with LeVar: "Ice cream. hi there. What a beautiful day to be out in the park." LeVar speaks later, before the book, saying "that's what this book, *Feelings*, is all about. by Aliki." The book is read by Raima Evans, Jessica Mercado, Laura Haymann, Paul Sidoriak, Jonathan Rose, Timothy Oakes, Jerry David and Pamela Stogner.

Not in the podcast audio: a composer's session in which Kirk Nurock turns voices and gibberish into music, a visit with the gorilla Coco, who signs about her feelings, and the kids' book reviews. The audio includes one short group segment from Nurock's session in which children chant about a pet cat, Whiskers, and ends on "We'll miss you, Whiskers."

Content note: the audio includes the death of a pet cat and a scene of a child shouting in anger.
