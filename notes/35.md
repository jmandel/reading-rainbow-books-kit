## About the book

*Abiyoyo* is a story-song for a listener who likes to sing along. A little boy plays the ukulele, and the grownups in town tell him to take that noise somewhere else. His father is a magician who makes things vanish with a wave of his wand, and the tricks he plays on neighbors get the two of them sent to live at the edge of town. Then a giant from the old stories turns out to be real, and the whole village is in trouble.

The book is based on a South African lullaby and folk story. Publishers Weekly called it "a triumph of storytelling and art" and said it is "too rollicking and exciting to lull a child to sleep." Kirkus praised "Seeger's wry understatement coupled with Hays' outstanding illustrations" and called the giant "satisfyingly grotesque." It suits children who enjoy a big monster, a repeated chorus and a quick story.

## Author and illustrator

Pete Seeger (1919-2014) was born in New York City and became a singer, songwriter and social activist. With the Weavers he had a chart-topping recording of "Goodnight, Irene" in 1950, and in 1966 he and his wife Toshi founded the Hudson River Sloop Clearwater to protect the Hudson River. Publishers Weekly noted that he first told this story-song to his children more than 20 years before the book appeared. He later wrote a sequel with Paul DuBois Jacobs, *Abiyoyo Returns* (2001), also illustrated by Hays. His picture book *The Deaf Musicians* received the Schneider Family Book Award in 2007.

Michael Hays earned a BFA in illustration from the Rhode Island School of Design (1979). Publishers Weekly described *Abiyoyo* as his first picture book, and Kirkus called it "a splendid introduction for Hays." He has illustrated about 22 children's books, including *Jonathan and His Mommy* by Irene Smalls and *The Hundredth Name* by Shulamith Levey Oppenheim, and teaches illustration and design at Columbia College Chicago.

## In this episode

The podcast opens in the studio with LeVar saying that "all kinds of books can be used for storytelling," and that *Abiyoyo* is a book you can sing, written by folk singer Pete Seeger, who is there with him. Seeger explains how a lullaby for his kids turned into a story, then sings the chorus and reads and sings the whole book. The audio ends on its last line, "And that's the story of Abiyoyo."

Reader: Pete Seeger.

Not in the podcast audio: a Lionel Richie music video, a Run-DMC rap video, and a child reviewing *Blackberry Ink* come before the book. After it come a dance company rehearsing Beauty and the Beast, two more child reviews, animation set to music, and a Run-DMC rap about reading.

Content note: the giant is said to eat people and grab sheep and cows, villagers scream and faint, and the boy and his father must live at the edge of town.
