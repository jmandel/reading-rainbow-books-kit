## About the book

Nelli and Worthi are best friends and also two tiny succulents. Their days are made of napping in the sun and sipping water drops, until one morning Nelli wonders whether there is more to life. Worthi does not know the answer, but suggests they look in the forest, and the two set off to find Nelli's purpose.

The search is full of silly misunderstandings, including a running joke that a purpose is not a baked potato. Along the way the friends meet a magical frog, a wise stink bug, and a snail who joins them as a sidekick.

Kirkus called it "sweetly philosophical" and a "comical, thought-provoking story" that "will win hearts," while noting that its message about purpose is "delivered quite subtly, and kids may not pick up on it." Horn Book described it as a "philosophical picture book" in which two plants "search for meaning." Laura Frantz, a collection development librarian, called it "a pure delight" and a natural conversation starter. Kirkus lists it for ages 4 to 7.

## Author and illustrator

Sophie Diao wrote and illustrated this book. A bookseller's listing (Mr. B's Emporium) calls it her debut picture book and describes her as the New York Times bestselling illustrator of *I Am Golden*. She lives in the hills of Oakland, California, where she also hikes. Her website says *Tiny Troubles* grew out of small comics she has drawn since 2016.

A San Francisco Public Library event page describes her as an Oakland artist who has worked on many Google Doodles.

Children who enjoy her pictures can look for other books she has illustrated:

- *I Am Golden*, written by Eva Chen (2022)
- *Sarah and the Big Wave*, written by Bonnie Tsui (2021)
- *I Am the Wind*, written by Michael Karg (2020)
- *From Park to Playa: The Trails that Connect Us*, written by Nell Cross Beckerman (2024)

## In this episode

The host, Mychal Threets, welcomes listeners to the library and explains that a purpose is "kind of like a mission": an umbrella's is to keep you dry, a watch's is to tell the time, and a person's is a "deep down job" that brings joy. He compares finding yours to building a jigsaw puzzle, where the fun is in the building, then says the book will offer "a little help" from Nelli.

Chrissy Teigen and John Legend read the book, and the audio ends on its last line. Content note: Nelli voices mild worries about the ocean and lightning, and a frog is stuck and needs help.

Not in the podcast audio: a three-question recap quiz, then three children visiting a hair stylist, a photographer and a chef who makes French toast with strawberries and matcha cream. Children also build a "bee buffet" of seeds and a water station, after questions to two honeybee relocation experts, and share book picks.
