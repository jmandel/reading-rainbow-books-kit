## About the book

*Feelings* is a 1984 picture book that works like a catalog of emotions. Instead of one story, each page holds a crowd of small comic-strip pictures, sometimes as many as twenty, showing children who are happy, sad, shy, jealous, angry or proud. Two birds comment on the feelings shown. The situations are ones a toddler's family will know: a birthday party, the first day of school, a beloved pet who dies.

Booklist wrote that "Children often have difficulty articulating emotions. That fact is the underpinning for Aliki's catalog of feelings, be they happy, sad, or somewhere in between." The Santa Cruz Sentinel felt the book "will delight children because it validates their feelings."

Not every reviewer was won over. School Library Journal found the number of pictures on a page made the book confusing to read, and said girls were often chosen to show negative emotions such as envy while boys showed bravery. The Bulletin of the Center for Children's Books thought the illustrations were too small and used inconsistently.

## Author and illustrator

Aliki Brandenberg, who writes and draws as Aliki, wrote and illustrated *Feelings* herself. She was born Aliki Liacouras on September 3, 1929, in Wildwood Crest, New Jersey. Her parents came from Greece and taught her Greek before English. She graduated from the Philadelphia Museum School of Art in 1951, worked as a muralist and commercial artist, and married the author Franz Brandenberg in 1957. Her first book, *The Story of William Tell*, appeared in 1960. The couple moved to London in 1977.

*Feelings* won the Prix du Livre pour Enfants in Geneva, Switzerland, in 1987. Her *Marianthe's Story* received the Jane Addams Peace Prize in 1999.

Aliki has called her work "hard fun" and said, "I draw in order to breathe." Young listeners might look for her picture books *My Five Senses* (1962), *Manners* and *Mummies Made in Egypt*.

## In this episode

The podcast opens in a park: an ice cream vendor takes an order for a vanilla cone with two scoops and rainbow sprinkles. After a dispute over cutting in line for the merry-go-round, a voice says "I have feelings," and the scene explains that feelings can't be seen or touched but can be hurt with words. The lead-in ends on "a whole rainbow of feelings" being what the book *Feelings*, by Aliki, is about. The reading then follows, voiced by Raima Evans, Jessica Mercado, Laura Haymann, Paul Sidoriak, Jonathan Rose, Timothy Oakes, Jerry David and Pamela Stogner, and ends on "We'll miss you, Whiskers."

Content note: the audio includes a scary dragon story, a shouted angry outburst and the death of a pet.

Not in the podcast audio: a composer leading a group making voice and percussion sounds; Dr. Patterson and Coco the gorilla discussing Coco's sadness over the death of her kitten; and three children reviewing other books.
