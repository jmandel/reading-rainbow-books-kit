# Review: note 49 (Brush)

Audio, reader (Hector Elizondo), season/episode/air date and the three review books all check out against inaudio.py, master.tsv and wiki_episodes.txt. No {{cn}} / citation-needed tags in the raw wikitext of Pere_Calders or Carme_Solé_Vendrell.

## Findings

1. major: "*Brush* began as the Catalan picture book *Raspall*, which earned the Serra d'Or children's literature prize in 1982."
   Wrong: Raspall was first a short story, published in Full Català from October 1941 (Wikipedia Pere_Calders raw wikitext: "In October 1941, he began collaborating with Full Català, where he published some short stories such as Raspall"). The picture book (Solé Vendrell, 1981) came later; the 1982 Serra d'Or prize is for Raspall (awards table).

2. major: "went into exile in Mexico in 1939, working there as a graphic artist for more than two decades."
   Source says he lived in Mexico twenty-three years (returned 1962) and from 1943 worked at UTEHA as a graphic artist and technical draftsman until his return (about 19 years), after earlier jobs (photographer, publisher work). Not "more than two decades" as a graphic artist.

3. minor: "a burglar fights Salah's father in the dark."
   "In the dark" is not in the audio (inaudio.py: "saw his father fighting with a burglar" in the library).

4. minor: Also recommended lists titles only; INSTRUCTIONS require "*Title* by Author". Catalog (archive.org): What the Mailman Brought by Carolyn Craven, illustrated by Tomie dePaola; Jumanji by Chris Van Allsburg; Egg-Carton Zoo by Rudi Haas and Hans Blohm (credits roll: introduction by David Suzuki).

5. minor: "Not in the podcast audio" list omits the turkey-balloon puppeteering segment (full captions 10:12-10:53); incomplete rather than wrong.

6. minor: the TeachingBooks source supports only the translator credit for Brush; no other fact in the note depends on it.
