# Review of 116 (Fly Away Home)

Findings:

1. MAJOR. "Kirkus called it an outstanding presentation of a serious subject for young children."
   Wrong subject: Kirkus says "Like The Wall (1990), an outstanding presentation of a serious topic for young children", so the praise is for The Wall's comparison, not this book. Source: https://www.kirkusreviews.com/book-reviews/eve-bunting/fly-away-home-2/ . Kirkus does call this book a "poignant narrative" and says Himler "matches Bunting's understated text with gentle sensibility".

2. MAJOR. "Not in the podcast audio: a closing explanation that most homeless families stay in shelters rather than airports".
   inaudio.py ends: "(CUT after: - Most of the time when families are homeless, they don't live in airports. They...)". The sentence is cut off, so the shelter claim is invented. Also "Kirkus ... of the 1991 Clarion Books edition" is fine (Pub Date March 18, 1991; Clarion Books).

3. MINOR. "why homeless people try not to be noticed".
   Audio: "It's against the law to live in public places ... so homeless people spend a lot of their time and energy trying not to get caught." Wording should be "get caught"; also "why many families are poor" is not said; the lead-in says most families are homeless because they are poor.

4. MINOR. "before he began illustrating children's books in 1963".
   Encyclopedia.com lists "artist and illustrator, 1963-" and says his first children's book project was Glad Day (1972). Reword to "artist and illustrator from 1963".
