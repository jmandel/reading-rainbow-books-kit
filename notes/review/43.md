Audio, reader, review books, bio facts, Parents' Choice award (Encyclopedia.com: "Parents' Choice Award ... 1986, for Best Friends"), "more than 90" (Wikipedia, no {{cn}}), Regina Medal, Pinkerton dog inspiration, "a little bit autobiographical" all verified.

1. Severity: major
   Sentence: "Kathy's feelings are allowed to be messy: she is jealous, and says so."
   Problem: Distorts the text. Her mother tells her not to be jealous, and Kathy hides her anger ("I told her how much I'd missed her, but I didn't tell her how mad I'd [been]"). She calls Louise a traitor in narration, but does not say she is jealous.
   Evidence: inaudio.py 43: "Mom told me not to be jealous of Louise's new friends." ... "I didn't tell her how mad I'd".

2. Severity: minor
   Sentence: "Kathy's street becomes, in her words, a lonely desert."
   Problem: The reading says "our neighborhood turned into a lonely desert", not street.
   Evidence: inaudio.py 43.

3. Severity: minor
   Sentence: "Next, try *Pinkerton, Behave!*, *A Rose for Pinkerton* or *Can I Keep Him?*"
   Problem: Repeats *Pinkerton, Behave!* from the previous sentence; "Dogs run through his work" is broader than the source (Encyclopedia.com says Can I Keep Him? is about stray animals).
   Evidence: https://www.encyclopedia.com/arts/educational-magazines/kellogg-steven-1941

4. Severity: minor
   Sentence: "hands her over to a woman who recently lost her sight"
   Problem: Source says "recently blind within the past year or so"; "lost her sight" is a mild paraphrase. Acceptable but could use "recently blind".
