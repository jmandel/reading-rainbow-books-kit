## Findings

1. **Major.** Sentence: "He explains that Tikki and Ronda Barber, twins who were then NFL players, wrote the book..."
   Wrong: names spelled as in the auto-captions. The publisher/Kirkus record gives Tiki and Ronde Barber.
   Evidence: https://www.kirkusreviews.com/book-reviews/tiki-barber/game-day/ ("Game Day by Tiki Barber & Ronde Barber").

2. **Major.** Sentence: "It includes twin swimmers Ashley and Kristen Stein Gordon, ... and Daniel Thrush..."
   Wrong: surnames (and "Kristen", which the captions also render as "Chris") come only from auto-captions and could not be verified outside them.
   Evidence: transcript [09:46] "Ashley and Kristen Stein Gordon", [17:24] "Daniel Thrush"; no other source found.

3. **Minor.** Sentence: "competitive swimmers training for the Olympic trials".
   Wrong: stronger than the segment. Their coach says he thinks they are "good enough to try to do something at the Olympic trials"; the girls say they hope for a shot.
   Evidence: transcript [15:59]-[16:40].

4. **Minor.** Sentence: "Ronde told the Buccaneers' website that the events are based on true experiences".
   Wrong: the source is a Buccaneers.com feature quoting Ronde, not something he told the site.
   Evidence: https://www.buccaneers.com/news/barber-book-two-i-game-day-i-2173073 ("It's pretty close to real events").

5. **Major.** Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mia_Hamm listed for the *Winners Never Quit* author line.
   Wrong: the raw wikitext tags the sentence "the juvenile fiction book ''Winners Never Quit''.{{cn|date=September 2026}}". Not usable. The credits roll in the audio source does name Mia Hamm as author, so the claim stands on the YouTube upload.
   Evidence: https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Mia_Hamm&action=raw; transcript [27:32] "Winners never quit by Mia Ham".

6. **Minor.** Author section omits Robert Burleigh, who helped put the Barbers' story to paper (episodes.tsv credits "with Robert Burleigh").
   Evidence: Buccaneers.com article, "Author Robert Burleigh would again assist the Barbers in putting their story to paper."
