# Review of 39 (The Paper Crane)

Checked: inaudio.py 39, master.tsv, wiki_episodes.txt, Publishers Weekly page, raw wikitext of Molly_Bang and Boston_Globe-Horn_Book_Award (no {{cn}} tags in either).

1. critical - "The audio opens in the Japanese garden of Gasho of Japan, a restaurant in New York State, where LeVar's lead-in begins"
   Wrong: the audio text never mentions Gasho of Japan, a restaurant, New York State or a garden setting. Evidence: inaudio.py 39 span 1 begins "One of the most enjoyable parts of Japanese culture is the food..." and the full captions contain no "Gasho", "New York" or restaurant visit.
2. critical - "The lead-in refers to Japanese culture and the restaurant visit"
   Wrong: the lead-in refers to Japanese food and Mr. Yabafuchi's carving; no restaurant visit is mentioned in the audio.
3. major - "Not in podcast audio: a musician playing the Japanese koto; LeVar folding a leaf-shaped origami bookmark; a paper artist ... blue jeans at a New York City studio; kids' reviews of three books; and a Japanese festival drum group"
   Unsupported: none of koto, origami bookmark, blue jeans, New York City studio or drum group appear in the captions or the cited sources. Only the three review books are supported (wiki_episodes.txt: "Paper" Through the Ages; Perfect Crane; How My Parents Learned to Eat).
4. minor - "A third came later for When Sophie Gets Angry..."
   Follows the Boston Globe-Horn Book sentence, so it reads as a third Boston Globe award; Wikipedia (Molly_Bang) says she was a Caldecott runner-up three times, the third for Sophie (1999).
5. minor - "1986 picture book winner"
   Boston Globe-Horn Book list puts The Paper Crane under 1986; the Molly_Bang page says 1987. Keep the award list year but say "listed under 1986".
