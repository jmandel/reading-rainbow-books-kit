# Review: 22 The Patchwork Quilt

1. MAJOR. "a classroom at the Joseph Lee School in Dorchester, Massachusetts, makes a paper quilt with each child's own square"
   Wrong: the cut segment shows the students making a fabric quilt (needle and thread, cloth patches), not paper.
   Evidence: YouTube PySoz25qBYQ captions [12:16] "the blue and white cloth that I used", [11:52] "using a needle and thread"; [13:03] "This quilt is made of individual patches sewn together with thread".
2. MINOR. "The same review called the text 'thoroughly platitudinous'"
   Kirkus's sentence has the book as subject ("It is bound to be admired, despite (or because of) being thoroughly platitudinous"), not specifically the text.
   Evidence: https://www.kirkusreviews.com/book-reviews/a/valene-flournoy/the-patchwork-quilt/
3. MINOR. Sources lists three libcat.library.tamu.edu records that fail to load (Cloudflare challenge) and supplied no checked fact.
   Review-book authors were instead confirmed from the episode credits and archive.org catalog records (Surat, Michele Maria; Rosenberg, Maxine B; Aliki).
