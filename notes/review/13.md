# Review of 13 (Arthur's Eyes)

Audio, reader (Bill Cosby), review books, and cut-segment descriptions all check out against inaudio.py, master.tsv, wiki_episodes.txt and the full captions. Wikipedia raw text for Marc Brown has no {{cn}} tags.

1. MAJOR. Sentence: 'Kirkus Reviews called the book a sequel to *Arthur's Nose*, "Executed with Brown's customary brash comic energy."'
   Kirkus never says "sequel" (it says Arthur's Nose identified Arthur as an aardvark), and the review is mixed but was presented as praise: it calls the story an old one with "many predecessors" and says "this does make the problem less than usually that of a sensitive odd-child-out."
   Evidence: https://kirkusreviews.com/book-reviews/marc-brown-6/arthurs-eyes

2. MAJOR. Sentence: "He later executive produced the *Arthur* television series and is a three-time Daytime Emmy Award winner."
   Wikipedia says executive producer for seasons ten through twenty-five only, and "three-time Emmy award winner" (not Daytime-specific in that sentence).
   Evidence: https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Marc_Brown_(author)&action=raw

3. MINOR. Sentence: "the kind of slapstick young listeners tend to enjoy" / "The humor carries it". Unsourced claim about reception.

4. MINOR. Sullivan/Bourke authorship for *A Show of Hands* was not in any listed source; confirmed at archive.org (creators Sullivan, Mary Beth; Bourke, Linda); source added.
