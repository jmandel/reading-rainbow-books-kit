## About the book

Ludlow seems to have been born frowning. Everyone noticed right away, and it was not a cute dimple or an adorable nose. As he grew, it stayed the same: it opened for food, an occasional burp and plenty of grumbling. He works in a complaint department and goes home grumpier than ever.

Then one night he has a dream, the funniest dream in the world, and he laughs in his sleep so loudly that the whole neighborhood hears. Once they hear it, nobody can help laughing along. Ludlow wakes up as grouchy as ever, but the dreams keep coming, and the laughter spreads from the town to a local radio station and beyond.

The book suits toddlers and preschoolers who enjoy big noises, grumbling and giggling. Its central idea is easy to follow: a laugh is catching. The deadpan narration also gives a child plenty of room to join in.

## Author and illustrator

Jon Agee wrote and illustrated *Ludlow Laughs*. He grew up in Nyack, New York, just up the street from the Hudson River, in a house where an art project was always under way. His first published drawing, "The Rat Race," ran on the New York Times Op Ed page while he was still in high school. He studied painting, sculpture and filmmaking at the Cooper Union School of Art, and he lives in San Francisco with his wife, Audrey.

His first picture book, *If Snow Falls*, began as a two-sentence story. *Ludlow Laughs* came after *Ellsworth*, about a dog who teaches economics. In his own account, the book "was doing very poorly" until Phyllis Diller read it on *Reading Rainbow*, and it then stayed in print for over twenty years.

Agee has since written many picture books, including *Dmitri the Astronaut* and *Little Santa*, and a series of wordplay books that begins with the palindromes in *Go Hang a Salami! I'm a Lasagna Hog!*
