This is a '''list of ''[[Reading Rainbow]]'' episodes''', hosted by longtime executive producer [[LeVar Burton]]. The show premiered on [[PBS Kids|PBS]] on July 11, 1983.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wagstaff |first=Keith |date=2014-06-03 |title=Even LeVar Burton can’t get the ‘Reading Rainbow’ song out of his head |url=http://www.today.com/popculture/even-levar-burton-cant-get-reading-rainbow-song-out-his-2D79749040 |access-date=2023-06-19 |website=[[Today (American TV program)|Today]] |language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Stone |first=Madeline |date=2014-06-17 |title=Here's Why 'Reading Rainbow' Was The Best Kids' TV Show Ever |url=https://www.businessinsider.com/what-is-reading-rainbow-2014-6 |access-date=2023-06-19 |website=[[Business Insider]] |language=en-US}}</ref> The final episode aired on November 10, 2006, reruns ceased on August 28, 2009. On June 20, 2012, an app for the show was released. On October 4, 2025, the show was revived, now hosted by [[Mychal Threets]].
